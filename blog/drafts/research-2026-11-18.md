### #1 Evergreen: "How to stop tapping 'Ignore Limit' in Screen Time (and why a passcode you know is not enough)"
- Target query: screen time ignore limit / how to remove ignore limit screen time. Parent query: does screen time work. BreakOff only mentions "Ignore Limit" in passing in two posts; no dedicated page.
- Why it can win: the current answers are competitor blogs (Detox, Nov 10, 2026; Tech Lockdown, updated Jun 4, 2026). Neither covers adults managing their own phone with someone else, which is BreakOff's angle, and the iOS 27 change gives a fresh reason to publish.
- Facts to use (both sources opened and agree for iOS 16 to 26): set a Screen Time passcode, then turn on "Block at End of Limit" for each App Limit; the toggle only appears with a passcode; after the limit you get one more minute, then the passcode is required. Detox: https://detox.so/blog/screen-time-ignore-limit . Tech Lockdown: https://www.techlockdown.com/articles/fix-ignore-limit-screen-time . Tech Lockdown alone adds that changing the passcode or a limit's schedule can switch the setting off (attribute to them). For iOS 27: Apple's own Screen Time page describes Time Allowances and does not mention App Limits or Block at End of Limit: https://support.apple.com/en-lamr/108806 (opened). Say that plainly and do not invent iOS 27 steps.
- BreakOff tie-in: Hardcore mode (no emergency exits) and the uninstall guard, as described in your Oct 13 review reply; and the shared break, where the "passcode" is effectively the group. Do not claim a Hardcore block cannot be edited unless you confirm it.
- Internal links: /blog/does-screen-time-work-blockers-vs-reminders/, /blog/apple-screen-time-loophole-app-categories/, /blog/lock-yourself-out-of-instagram-iphone/, /app-blocker-for-kids/ (the Family Sharing version of the steps), /family-screen-time-app/.
- Hot take: if you set the passcode yourself, Block at End of Limit just turns a one-tap excuse into a four-digit one. Give the key to someone else, or share the break.
