## Hot take angle (for rank 1)

Apple's Screen Time redesign proves the point we keep making: Apple thinks screen time is a kid problem. Time Allowances, Ask to Browse, Child Accounts. All of it assumes a parent holding the keys. But the average American adult checks their phone 352 times a day. Nobody is parenting us. The hard part was never the wall, it was wanting to climb it. Apple builds walls. The real fight is motivation, and that is exactly the gap third-party apps live in. Also worth saying out loud: Apple ships this stuff every June and screen time keeps going up. Maybe the OS vendor whose revenue depends on engagement is not the right one to solve this.
