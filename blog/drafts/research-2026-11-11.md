### 3.1 AlternativeTo (alternativeto.net)
- What: crowd-sourced "alternatives to X" directory. It shows up for "opal alternative" and "brick alternative" in this week's searches, and there is an "Opal Focus Alternatives" page with several pages of entries: https://alternativeto.net/software/opal-focus/
- Route (verified in the FAQ, https://alternativeto.net/faq, opened): sign up, click the user icon top right, "Suggest new application", fill platforms, license, description, tags, submit. Approval is at admin discretion, usually a couple of days up to a week. App must support English and be released. No fee is mentioned. Rules: no promotion via user profiles, no fake likes. An older how-to (https://buttondown.com/where-to-post/archive/submitting-on-alternativetonet, 2020) says you can import data from the iOS App Store and suggest alternatives to your own app; add Opal, Brick, one sec, ScreenZen, Freedom, ClearSpace as alternatives.
- How competitors are listed: Opal has its own page with alternatives under it. I could not open individual small-app pages (site returned errors), so I cannot describe their entries.

## 4. Reviews and trust

- BreakOff: no new reviews. Praise themes: "just difficult enough", shared breaks at dinner and on vacation. Complaint: blocks editable or deletable, price vs free blockers.
- Brick (opened): praise for blocks that "can't be overridden" and for not being intrusive like Opal; complaint (May 17) that scheduled unbricking broke again. Lesson: reliability of schedules is a trust point; BreakOff copy can promise schedules only as far as they are tested.
- Freedom (opened): praise for uninstall protection and locked settings; complaints that protection sometimes stops working until reboot, and that users wait out a block by switching to other distractions.
- ClearSpace (opened): complaints about inaccurate usage totals and limits triggering when not reached; one reviewer compares it unfavourably to one sec and ScreenZen.
- Common thread across all four: people want blocks that hold, and they notice fast when they do not. BreakOff's honest angle is that a shared break adds a social reason not to cheat, on top of Hardcore.
- Honest review chances: in-app rating prompt after a completed shared break; Indie App Circle testers for feedback (not reviews); creators from earlier briefs.
