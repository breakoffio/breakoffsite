Opal Alternatives: 6 iPhone App Blockers With Real Prices

November 12, 2026 · 6 min read

The best Opal alternatives for iPhone are ScreenZen (free), one sec ($19.99 a year), Freedom ($39.99 a year), ClearSpace ($6.99 a month), Brick (free app plus a $59 device) and BreakOff (free blocking, Pro $49.99 a year). Opal costs $99.99 a year, so every option here is cheaper. Pick by what happens when you try to quit early.

We checked the prices below on the US App Store this week. A lot of roundups quote prices loosely, and these apps change them often. So here is what we actually found, plus our honest take on what makes a blocker stick.

How much does Opal cost in November 2026?

Opal is a good app. It holds 4.7 stars from about 91,000 ratings on the App Store, and it just shipped version 4.23. It is also the priciest app in this comparison: $99.99 a year, $19.99 a month, or $4.99 a week.

That price is why most people go looking for an alternative. Fair enough. We just think price is the wrong reason to switch, and we will get to why.

Why do people really quit app blockers?

Here is our take. Most people leave Opal over price. But price is not why blockers fail. Blockers fail because you are the only one holding the key.

Reading the App Store reviews for Brick, Freedom and ClearSpace this week, the same thing kept coming up. People want blocks that hold, and they notice fast when they do not.

A cheaper app with the same escape hatch gives you the same 11pm. Switching to a $20 blocker fixes your bill, not your habit. So before you compare features, ask one question: what happens when I try to quit early? We dug into the research behind that in our look at whether app blockers work.
