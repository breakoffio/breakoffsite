Break Modes

Five ways
to lock in

Every break has a mode that decides how it ends. Flexible trusts you, I'm Done doesn't have to. Pick the level of commitment that matches your day.

Flexible

Quit whenever, no judgment

Pick your apps, set a length, and start. Your distracting apps disappear while the timer runs, but the door stays open — pause or end the break whenever you want. The easiest way to start building the habit.

Locked

Locked in, break glass if needed

Start it and it runs its full length — there is no end button. For true emergencies there's a break-glass escape, deliberately uncomfortable so you never use it on autopilot. And the Phone app is never blocked, so calls always work.

QR Code

Same scan in, same scan out

Scan any QR code or barcode to start the break — print one, or use a code already sitting in your house: a coffee bag, a poster, a piece of mail. The only thing that ends the break is scanning that exact code again. Leave it on the fridge and your apps stay blocked until you walk to the fridge. Nothing to buy, and setup takes a minute.

NFC Tag

Same tap in, same tap out

Hold your phone to a standard NFC sticker and the break starts. Tap the same one to end it — no camera needed. NTAG stickers run about a dollar each: stick one on your desk, the bedroom doorframe, your gym bag, or the front door, and distance does the willpower for you.

I'm Done

Done-done, until midnight

One tap locks your distracting apps for the rest of the day. No pause, no undo — they come back at midnight. The strongest commitment BreakOff offers, for the nights you already know how the scroll would have gone.
