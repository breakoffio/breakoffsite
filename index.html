THE HABIT

Decide once. Then let it run.

ROUTINES
Rules that run on their own

Pick the days, the times and the apps. Start from Work Focus, Slow Morning, Better Sleep, School Focus, Family Time or Weekend Reset, and change anything you like. Skip the next one and your streak stays where it is.

Family screen time app · Phone-free family time ideas

CIRCLES
Be more present, together

Circles are the people you take breaks with. Send an invite and your phones go quiet at the same time. Together This Week shows the streaks across your Circle. Ages 16 and up.

HOST
Put the whole room on a break

One poster, one QR code. Everyone who scans it starts the same break on their own phone. Change the time or the name and the same poster keeps working. You see a count, never names. Host is part of Pro.

STREAKS AND HISTORY
See the habit taking shape

Every break fills in a day on the calendar. Not a score to chase, just a quiet record of the time you have been getting back.
